# Data preparation

## Overview

The data preparation work focused on making the simulated operational datasets consistent and suitable for exploratory analysis and later SQL and modelling work. The operational data covers 1 January to 31 December 2025 and consists of 15 related datasets generated by the project script.

## Data loading and preparation

The generated operational datasets were loaded into Python from pickle files for the analysis workflow. Preparation included reviewing the available datasets, checking their structures and relationships, and preparing the sales records for analysis.

For the sales analysis:

- Voided transactions were excluded from completed-sales calculations.
- Transaction item records were joined to transaction headers, product information and branch information.
- Transaction dates were converted to datetime values for time-based analysis.
- Cost of goods sold and gross profit before refunds were calculated at transaction-line level.
- Refunds were kept separate from sales and gross profit before refunds.
- Customer-level analysis was restricted to completed transactions with a recorded customer ID.

The project distinguishes the source CSV name `opening_inventory.csv` from the PostgreSQL table name `open_inventory`. The latter is the table name used in the database schema.

## Customer and sales analysis rules

Around 80% of transaction headers were designed to have a customer ID; approximately 20% have no customer ID. Customer-level measures therefore represent identified customers and should not be generalised to all transactions.

Voided transactions are excluded from sales analysis. Refunds are recorded separately, so gross profit reported before refunds should not be described as return-adjusted gross profit.

## Public contextual data

The Kenya monthly CPI series and 2025 public holiday calendar were maintained separately from the synthetic operational datasets. They were not generated by the operational data-generation script.
